# Smart Grocery List

## 🛒 Why I Built This

Shopping on a budget can be stressful. I found myself constantly:
- Mentally calculating totals while shopping to avoid overspending
- Losing track of how much I've spent mid-trip
- Worrying whether I could afford items I was about to put in my cart
- Getting home and discovering I'd overspent

**Smart Grocery List** solves this by automatically tracking your spending in real-time, so you always know your budget and can shop with confidence.

## ✨ Key Features

- Real-time budget tracking
- Saving and Open Lists for future references

## 🚀 Getting Started

The Smart Grocery List is a simple .exe. So, Just Click and start using.
